01 · The experience

A storefront for technology and better builds.

Volt Market is a responsive electronics-store demonstration for Moroccan shoppers exploring PC components, televisions, monitors, and accessories. Its interface is designed around clear product information, practical comparison, and an approachable PC-building flow.

Look and feel

A dark, premium technology aesthetic is the default, with a light-theme switch and a focused electric-blue accent. Product photography, clean type, generous spacing, responsive layouts, visible focus states, and subtle interaction feedback help the store feel clear on both desktop and mobile.

02 · Discover and decide

Find the right part, then check the details.

Suggestions update as you type. Keyboard arrows move through suggestions, Enter opens a match, and Ctrl/Cmd+K focuses search.

Image gallery, pricing and discount display, availability, specifications, sample ratings/reviews, related products, and add-to-cart controls.

Other pages

03 · Build and buy

Tools that carry a shopper from idea to order.

PC Builder

Choose components and see a running total. The compatibility check compares the CPU socket with the motherboard socket and flags a match or mismatch.

Choose CPU→Choose motherboard→Check socket match→Review build total

Compatibility is intentionally limited to CPU-to-motherboard socket matching. It does not check every fit, power, or clearance requirement.

Stock checks

  • Product and cart pages show the available catalog quantity.
  • Add-to-cart and quantity controls stop a cart line from exceeding the shown stock.
  • Checkout fetches the latest catalog again before creating the demo order and blocks quantities that are no longer available.

Availability is sample data. Checkout does not reserve inventory or contact a retailer.

Cart and checkout

Edit quantities, remove products, apply the demonstration VOLT10 promo code, and review a delivery and payment-method selection. A valid demo checkout saves an illustrative order in this browser and displays a confirmation and tracking code.

Payment boundary: no card details are collected, no payment is processed, and no real order is transmitted. Cash-on-delivery and card choices are demonstration selections only.
04 · Languages and accessibility

One store, three interface languages.

The interface includes English, French, and Arabic translations. Choosing Arabic switches the page direction to right-to-left, including navigation and localized layouts. Prices use Moroccan dirhams (MAD).

Practical accessibility details

  • Controls have labels, keyboard focus styling, and accessible names.
  • Product images include descriptive alternative text where they convey product information.
  • Availability and errors are communicated with words as well as color.
  • Responsive layouts support mobile and desktop browsing.
  • Search suggestions can be navigated from the keyboard.

Customer-service touchpoints

The footer includes click-to-call and WhatsApp links, plus routes for contact, FAQs, warranty/returns, and order tracking. Replace the example contact details and demo policy copy with verified business information before launch.

Translation note: the language selector changes the storefront interface and RTL presentation. Product titles and descriptions remain catalog content and should be translated separately if a fully localized catalog is needed.
05 · Owner and staff administration

Manage products and team access.

The private admin panel at /admin is built around an owner account configured through private environment settings. The owner can manage the catalog and create or remove staff logins.

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerManage products, create and remove staff accounts, and see team online/last-seen status.
StaffSign in with an individual account and manage product catalog records. They cannot administer team accounts.

Team status and account safety

  • “Online” means the signed-in admin page checked in during the last 75 seconds. Otherwise the owner sees the last activity time.
  • Staff passwords are stored as one-way scrypt hashes; they cannot be revealed again from the panel.
  • Removing a staff login revokes its sessions immediately.
  • Admin sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies with an expiry. Login attempts are throttled.

Product controls

Search the catalog and add, edit, or remove products, including brand, category, price, stock, image, specifications, and filtering attributes. Changes save to SQLite and appear in the storefront catalog.

Security reminder: never put owner or staff passwords in this guide or share them publicly. Configure private credentials on the server and share staff access privately.

Run locally

  1. Install Node.js 20.9 or later.
  2. In the project folder, install dependencies with npm install.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env.local and set an owner username, a unique password of at least 12 characters, and a random signing secret of at least 32 characters.
  4. Start the site with npm run dev.
  5. Visit http://localhost:3000; open http://localhost:3000/admin for administration.

Generate a private session secret

node -e "console.log(require('node:crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('hex'))"

Use the command output as ADMIN_SESSION_SECRET in your private .env.local file. Do not publish that file.

Persistence and hosting

Set VOLT_DATABASE_PATH to a writable persistent local volume for deployment, and back up the SQLite database to keep product edits and staff accounts. An ephemeral/serverless filesystem is not suitable for this SQLite setup.

07 · Scope and next steps

A complete storefront demonstration, with honest boundaries.

Volt Market provides a working frontend and local catalog/admin foundation. Connecting real commerce services is a separate production step.

Included

Browse and search products, filter and compare, save a wishlist, build a socket-checked PC, edit an owner-managed catalog, and issue staff logins with presence status.

Demonstration only

Prices, stock, ratings, reviews, countdown, and contact details are illustrative. Cart and demo orders are local to the browser.

Before a real store launch

  • Replace sample catalog data with verified products, prices, stock, and properly licensed product imagery.
  • Connect a backend order workflow, inventory reservations, delivery handling, and customer notifications.
  • Choose a compliant payment provider before accepting card payments; this demo never processes money.
  • Publish verified contact, warranty, privacy, and returns information.
  • Deploy with secure private environment settings, persistent database storage, backups, and HTTPS.
